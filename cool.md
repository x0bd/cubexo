# CubeXO - Cool Feature Brainstorm

Here's a list of potential cool and unique features we could explore for CubeXO, moving beyond a simple voxel converter.

## I. Enhancing Voxel Artistry & Customization

1.  **Advanced Voxelization Controls:**

    -   **Voxel Density Slider:** Allow users to dynamically change the resolution of the voxelization in real-time (or with a quick re-process) to see different levels of abstraction.
    -   **Color Palette Control:**
        -   **Quantization:** Limit the voxel model to a specific number of colors (e.g., 8, 16, 32) for a retro look.
        -   **Palette Import/Presets:** Allow users to upload a color palette image or choose from pre-defined artistic palettes (e.g., "Sunset," "Neon," "Forest").
        -   **Dominant Color Extraction:** Option to base the voxel colors on the N most dominant colors of the original model.
    -   **Material Properties for Voxels:** Beyond color, let users assign properties like "metallic," "emissive" (glowing), or even slight "transparency" to all voxels or based on original material.
    -   **Voxelization Styles:**
        -   **Hollow Mode:** Only voxelize the outer shell of the model, making it look like a hollow sculpture.
        -   **Fragmented/Exploded Voxelization:** An option where voxels are slightly offset or "exploded" outwards from their original positions for an abstract effect.

2.  **Post-Voxelization Editing (Voxel Sculpting & Painting):**
    -   **Voxel Paint Tools:** Allow users to click on individual voxels (or use a brush) to change their color.
    -   **Add/Remove Voxels:** Simple tools to click and add a new voxel or remove an existing one, enabling small tweaks and creative additions.
    -   **Symmetry Mode:** For editing, mirror changes across X, Y, or Z axes.

## II. Dynamic & Interactive Experiences

3.  **Interactive Voxel Effects:**

    -   **"Voxel Shaker":** On mouse drag or a button press, make the voxels "jiggle" or temporarily disperse before reforming, like a snow globe.
    -   **"Reactive Bloom":** Voxels could subtly glow or change color when the mouse hovers over them or parts of the model.
    -   **Click-to-Explode/Reform:** A satisfying effect where clicking the model makes its voxels fly apart and then elegantly reassemble.
    -   **Audio-Reactive Voxels:** (More advanced) The model's voxels could pulse, scale, or change color in time with music or microphone input.

4.  **Animation & Motion:**
    -   **Turntable GIF/Video Export:** Easily export a rotating animation of the current voxel model.
    -   **"Build-Up" Animation:** Animate the voxels appearing sequentially to construct the model, or dissolving away. This could be a cool loading animation or an exportable effect.
    -   **Simple Keyframed Voxel Animations:** Allow users to set a few key states (e.g., a part rotated, a section moved) and have the app tween the voxels.

## III. Scene, Environment & Sharing

5.  **Enhanced Scene & Lighting Control:**

    -   **Environment Presets:** Offer different background colors, simple gradient skyboxes, or even image-based lighting (HDRI) presets to dramatically change the mood. (e.g., "Studio Softbox," "Sunset Glow," "Cyberpunk Night").
    -   **Ground Plane Options:** Toggle a reflective ground plane, or different grid styles.
    -   **Adjustable Shadow Intensity/Softness:** More control over how shadows look.

6.  **"Voxel Diorama" Mode:**

    -   Allow users to upload and voxelize a few models and arrange them in a small scene.
    -   Basic manipulation (move, rotate, scale) of each voxelized model within the scene.

7.  **Unique Import Options:**
    -   **Image-to-Voxel Relief:** Upload a 2D image and convert it into a 3D voxel relief based on brightness or color values.
    -   **Text-to-Voxel:** Input text and have it rendered as 3D voxel lettering.

## IV. User Experience & Polish

8.  **Undo/Redo Stack:** Crucial for any editing or customization features.
9.  **User Model Library:** If users upload models, save their voxelized versions locally in the browser for quick re-access.
10. **AR Export:** Allow exporting models in a format like USDZ for viewing in Augmented Reality on compatible devices.

_These ideas are for brainstorming and future development direction._
